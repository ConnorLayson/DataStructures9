In big data sets, it is a fast and reliable way to get specific data sets. 
Even when the data has the same hash, it is easy to point down a line of the data
to the right set, like a small list that we had before. It is sort of like a list
of lists, where each list has it's own name. So it's a find the first name, then 
go down that list to the actuall name. 

I handled collisions by pointing down the nodes and assigning the conflicting 
to the bottom of the chain.

A programer may choose to use a hash table when they need fast, constant-time access,
storing unique keys, or when you care about lookup speed more than order. Like with
a contact list like in the assignment, an inventory system, or something similar.
